## 🌿 The Design

Botanforce Lite was a **test case** for the [Botanforce](https://github.com/ReinTanto/Botanforce). Before committing to the CNC-machined aluminium build, the fitment and tolerances of the **Realforce R2 plate** and the **custom USB-C daughterboard** needed to be verified with a cheaper, faster material — so a stacked acrylic case was laser-cut instead.

It also served as a **tracing logo test**: the "BOTANFORCE" nameplate was traced out and cut into the acrylic to check how the logo would read on the final build.

The cutting files went through two revisions (**revisi 1** and **revisi 2**) as tolerances were dialed in.

---

## 🔨 The Build

- **January 27, 2023** — the cutting DXFs were finalized (revisi 1 → revisi 2) and sent to the laser cutter.
- **January 2023** — the acrylic case was laser-cut, stacked, and assembled with the stock R2 plate and the custom USB-C daughterboard. Fitment and tolerances verified.
